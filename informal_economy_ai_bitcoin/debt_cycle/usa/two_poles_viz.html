The US, late-cycle

Debt near a post-WWII record, a structural peacetime deficit, an interest bill compounding faster than growth, and the reserve privilege eroding at the margin. Because the US borrows in its own reserve currency, the resolution is inflation and slow privilege-loss — not external default.

The Fed's lever — how a rate hike transmits

The US has the policy space Nigeria lacks. A monetary VAR (1960–2025, recursive identification) traces a +100bp fed-funds shock: output falls fast, unemployment rises with a lag, inflation eases last — after a brief "price-puzzle" tick (a known VAR artifact).
