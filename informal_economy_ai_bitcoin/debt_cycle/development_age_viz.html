Why the parallel is unearned

The income match is arithmetic; the outcome depends on initial conditions. On the seven catch-up determinants, Nigeria today is adverse versus the escapers at the same stage — and the factors compound (oil rents both corrode institutions and crowd out manufacturing).

Structural-readiness index  0/100  · 7 of 7 factors adverse (demographics the one latent upside)

Nigeria sits at Korea-1970's income, but its path tilts toward the Brazil stall, not the Korea escape — and it isn't matching Brazil yet: per-capita income has fallen since 2010.

The escaper template was 35–45% savings, export manufacturing, and low debt → income doubling in ~8–10 years. Nigeria runs ~15–20% savings, manufacturing ~8–9% of GDP (falling), and is urbanizing without industrializing. Escape requires security, institutions, fertility, human capital, and a manufacturing jobs-ladder to flip together — the same governance swing-factor the debt-cycle tracker already flags as the top bear trigger.
