# Nigeria Public Debt — Debt Stock & External Profile

**Compiled:** 2026-06-30 · **Coverage:** annual year-end 2015–2025 (2026 not yet published) + quarterly Q1'2023–Q4'2025
**Scope of "Total Public Debt" (DMO definition):** Federal Government (FGN) + 36 States + FCT, external + domestic. This scope is consistent across the whole series (states/FCT have been included throughout).

**Flag legend:** `[SOURCED]` = from a cited publication · `[SOURCED-2nd]` = official DMO figure reported via a secondary outlet (DMO portal/PDF not directly re-extracted this session due to anti-scraping block; uncontroversial, widely reported) · `[ESTIMATED]` = derived/computed by analyst (method noted) · `[PROVISIONAL]` = latest data, may be revised · `[BLANK]` = not reliably sourced, left empty.

> **Important currency note:** USD values look volatile because the naira floated in June 2023 (~N460→~N770/$) and was devalued again through 2024 (to ~N1,500–1,650/$). So 2024 USD total *falls* even as naira total *rises*. Both currencies are reported. DMO converts each quarter at its own period-end rate.

## 7. Debt-to-GDP Ratio — DMO vs IMF (the two differ; 2025 rebasing break)

> **MAJOR SERIES BREAK:** In early 2025 the NBS **rebased GDP** (new base year 2019; added digital economy, fintech, creative & informal sectors), sharply raising nominal GDP. This **mechanically cut the debt/GDP ratio** with no change in debt. DMO's end-2024 ratio of **52.13% (old GDP)** dropped to **39.4% at Q1'2025 (rebased GDP)**. Ratios before/after Q1'2025 are **not directly comparable**.

| Q1'2025 / 2025 | **39.4%** (rebased; 20.77% domestic + 18.63% external) | **36.4%** (2025); IMF also cites ~32.3% | [SOURCED] | DMO via Nairametrics 2025-07-22; IMF |

> **Why DMO and IMF differ:** (a) **coverage** — IMF "general government gross debt" includes items like AMCON, CBN overdrafts/Ways&Means and other public-sector liabilities beyond the DMO's FGN+states+FCT marketable/loan stock; (b) **GDP denominator and timing** of the rebasing adoption differ between the two; (c) IMF uses calendar-year average GDP, DMO uses period figures. Treat the two series as **parallel, not interchangeable.**

## Notes, Caveats & Series Breaks

1. **Q2'2023 structural break (largest):** Total debt jumped ~75% QoQ (N49.85trn → N87.38trn). Two causes: (i) inclusion of **N22.712trn of securitized CBN "Ways & Means" advances** into the public-debt stock for the first time, and (ii) the **June-2023 naira float**. Pre-Q2'2023 totals exclude the Ways & Means stock — do not compare levels across this break without adjustment.
2. **2024 devaluation distortion:** Naira total rose +48.6% in 2024 while USD total *fell* (to $94.23bn) — almost entirely an FX revaluation effect on the external (USD) portion. External *share* of total spiked to 48.6% for the same reason.
3. **2025 GDP rebasing:** affects all ratio series (Section 7), not the debt levels (Sections 1–2).
4. **Scope consistency:** DMO "Total Public Debt" = FGN + 36 States + FCT, domestic + external, throughout 2015–2025 (no scope break for states inclusion in this window).
5. **2015–2018 annual figures** are official DMO year-end numbers reported via secondary outlets; the DMO portal/Statistical-Bulletin PDFs return HTTP 406/HTML to automated fetches, so they were not directly re-extracted this session. Naira figures are firm; **USD year-end values for 2016–2018 are approximate** and should be cross-checked against the DMO Public Debt Statistical Bulletin if precision to two decimals is required.
6. **Q2/Q3 2024 USD totals** are analyst estimates (NGN total ÷ DMO period-end rate); external USD for those quarters ($42.90bn, $43.03bn) are DMO-sourced.
7. **2031 Eurobond split** ($1.0bn + $0.70bn) is from a DMO PDF whose column alignment was partially garbled on extraction; the ~$1.7bn aggregate and the individual coupons/dates are reliable, but verify the exact per-bond face values against the DMO Eurobond table if needed.
