# Nigeria Private Equity & Venture Capital — 2015-2026

**Scope:** Annual PE deal activity and VC/startup funding into Nigeria, with African context, notable mega-rounds/unicorns, fundraising/dry powder, DFI participation, and sizing vs FDI.
**Compiled:** 2026-06-30. **Sourcing flags:** [SOURCED] = directly from a named publication; [ESTIMATED] = derived/approximated; [PROVISIONAL] = partial-year or preliminary. Blank cells = not sourceable, left intentionally blank — NOT guessed.

> **CRITICAL METHODOLOGY WARNING.** Trackers disagree materially and this is the single biggest data-quality issue in this file:
> - **Different trackers, different totals.** Partech Africa, AVCA, Briter Bridges, Disrupt Africa, and Africa: The Big Deal each define "African/Nigerian startup," deal eligibility, disclosure thresholds, and instrument scope differently. For the same year they can differ 2-3x (e.g., 2017 Africa total: Partech $560M vs Disrupt Africa $195M).
> - **Equity-only vs equity+debt+grants.** From ~2023 onward, venture debt became large (41% of African capital in 2025 per Partech). A "Nigeria $186M" headline (equity-only, one tracker) and a "Nigeria $572M" headline (equity+debt, Partech) describe the *same year (2025)* — they are not contradictory, they measure different things.
> - **"Africa" totals are routinely mis-cited as "Nigeria."** This file labels every figure as Africa-wide vs Nigeria-specific. Treat any uncited "Nigeria raised $X billion" claim with suspicion.
> - **PE vs VC.** AVCA "African Private Capital Activity" blends PE, VC, infrastructure, and private debt. "Venture Capital in Africa" is the VC-only cut. They are different denominators — do not mix.

**Fintech share:**
- 2019: Nigeria received **66% of all African fintech investment** [SOURCED, Partech].
- 2021: Fintech ≈ **73.5%** of Nigeria's startup funding ($741.6M of ~$1.09B; e-commerce 13.8% / $139.2M) [SOURCED, Techpoint].

**Funding-winter narrative:** 2021 peak → Q3 2022 the global downturn reached Africa (lag vs rest of world) → 2023 was the trough (Nigeria −59% to $468M) → 2024-2025 stabilisation/partial rebound (Africa $4.1B in 2025, but driven by **debt**, not equity; equity still soft). Nigeria specifically rebounded modestly in 2024 (#1) then slipped to #4 in 2025. [SOURCED]

**Interpretation (analyst note):**
- Nigeria's **VC inflow (~$520M in 2024, Partech) is on the same order of magnitude as — and in some years exceeds — recorded FDI proper** (FDI was ~$377M in full-year 2023 per NBS; ~$149M in H1 2024). This makes startup/VC capital a non-trivial share of genuine "new money" equity into Nigeria, even though it is dwarfed by FPI (hot money) and by total capital importation.
