# Nigeria — Financial Inclusion, Digital Payments & Diaspora Remittances

**Compiled:** 2026-06-30 · **Coverage:** survey-years/annual 2015–2026 where available
**Connects to:** informal-economy study (MSME/informal-sector share) + Big-Debt-Cycle external-flows (remittances as FX/BoP support)

**Flag legend:** `[SOURCED]` = from a cited primary publication · `[SOURCED-2nd]` = primary figure (CBN/EFInA/NIBSS) reported via a secondary outlet, original not re-extracted this session · `[ESTIMATED]` = derived/computed by analyst · `[PROVISIONAL]` = latest data, may be revised · `[BLANK]` = not reliably sourceable, left empty — NOT guessed.

> **CADENCE NOTE:** Financial-inclusion headline rates are **SURVEY-YEAR, not annual**. EFInA A2F runs biennially (…2014, 2016, 2018, 2020, **2023** — no 2022 round). World Bank Global Findex runs ~every 3–4 yrs (2014, 2017, 2021, **2025** [field-year 2024]). Do **not** interpolate intervening years.

> **METHODOLOGY BREAK:** EFInA's **2023** A2F adopted a *new methodology* (re-based adult population / sampling). EFInA restated 2020 on the new basis (formal 56%, excluded 32%), which differs from the **original 2020** A2F report (formal 50.5%, excluded 35.9%). Both are shown below; do not splice the two series without noting the break.

> **REMITTANCES — TWO DIFFERENT MEASURES (do not add):** (a) **World Bank "personal remittances, received"** = full BoP estimate incl. informal channels (~$17–24bn/yr). (b) **CBN IMTO inflows** = only formal International-Money-Transfer-Operator channel (~$3–5bn/yr). The 2024–25 "surge" headlines mostly refer to the *IMTO* channel (+44.5% in 2024) following CBN's 2024 IMTO/NAFEM reforms.
