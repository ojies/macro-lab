> **USD market-cap collapse despite naira ASI gains — the central observation:** The mid-2023 naira float (₦~460 → ₦900+ in H2-2023, then ₦1,500+ through 2024) meant the dollar value of the market FELL even as the naira ASI surged. ASI rose **+45.9%** in 2023 and **+37.65%** in 2024 in naira, yet USD market cap slid from ~$60bn (end-2022) to ~$45bn (end-2023) to ~$41bn (end-2024) — a roughly one-third dollar drawdown across two boom years in local terms. Only in 2025, as the naira stabilised (~₦1,460–1,535), did USD cap recover to **$68.7bn** on a +51% naira ASI year. Naira returns substantially overstate dollar wealth creation 2023–24.
