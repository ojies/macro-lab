The debt isn't the danger. The room to manage it is.

Five economies on one Dalio template. Nigeria carries the least debt and the most risk; Japan the most debt and a slow, managed grind. What separates them is policy space.

Debt level vs room to manage it

Government debt (horizontal) against the policy space to work it off (vertical — reserve status, monetary sovereignty, capital controls, fiscal capacity). The diagonal is the story: low-debt Nigeria sits in the danger corner because it has no space; high-debt US and Japan sit higher because they do.

Debt = general-government gross debt % of GDP (2025, IMF/Eurostat/FRED). Policy-space score is a qualitative composite (0–100) across currency sovereignty, reserve status, external position, and fiscal/administrative capacity. China's total non-financial debt is ~300% of GDP; the ~99% here is government only.
